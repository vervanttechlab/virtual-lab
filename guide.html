Install, sign in, and start your activities

The DAL3 Virtual Lab works on a phone and on a computer. Your trainer gives you three things: the class code, your Student ID and your PIN (usually by email).

1Open the link

Open the lab link in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone). The first time, use Wi-Fi if you can, so the whole app downloads to your phone.

2Install it on your phone

Installing puts a DAL3 Lab icon on your home screen, like a normal app. It also lets the lab work without a signal.

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots, top right).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen. On some phones it says Install app.
  4. Tap Install or Add.
  1. Open the link in Safari (installing does not work from Chrome on iPhone).
  2. Tap the Share button: a square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add at the top right.

From now on, open the lab from the DAL3 Lab icon on your home screen.

3Sign in

Type exactly what your trainer gave you, then tap Sign in:

Class codefor example DAL3-2026
Student IDfor example S014
PIN4 digits, keep it private

You stay signed in on that phone. Tap your Student ID at the top right to sign out.

4Do your activities

  • Choose a Day. Each day has a Lesson, a Practice workbook, the activities and a self-check quiz.
  • An activity shows CLOSED until your trainer opens it in class.
  • Inside an activity, switch between Task (instructions and your answers) and Workbook (the spreadsheet).
  • Select a yellow cell, type the formula in the bar at the top, and tap the check mark (or press Enter).
  • Tap Check to see which answers are correct. Wrong cells get a red border.
  • Tap Submit when you are done. Your trainer sees your work right away. You may submit again; the latest one counts.

5Using a computer

Open the same link in Chrome or Edge on any computer and sign in the same way. No installation is needed. On a computer the instructions and the workbook are side by side, and Excel shortcuts work: Ctrl + C, Ctrl + V, Ctrl + D (fill down), Ctrl + R (fill right), F4 (lock a reference) and Alt + = (AutoSum).

You can start on your phone and continue on a computer: your submitted work is saved on the class server. Work you have not submitted yet stays on the device where you typed it.
